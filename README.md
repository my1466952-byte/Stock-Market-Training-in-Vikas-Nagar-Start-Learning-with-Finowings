## 📈 Why Learn Stock Market Trading?

Understanding the stock market before making investment decisions is important. Beginners can start by learning how the Indian stock market works, including NSE, BSE, and demat accounts.

Key topics to explore include:

* **Technical Analysis:** Understand charts, candlestick patterns, support and resistance, and price action.
* **Fundamental Analysis:** Learn about company performance, financial statements, and financial ratios.
* **Options Trading:** Explore the basic concepts of options and their associated risks.
* **Risk Management:** Understand position sizing, risk control, and disciplined trading.
* **Trading Psychology:** Learn why patience and emotional discipline matter in financial markets.
